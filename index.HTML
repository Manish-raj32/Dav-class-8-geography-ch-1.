NOTES

1. 'Utility and value of a resource vary from time to time and place to place.' Give any three examples.

Answer:
1. Water is more valuable in deserts than in areas with heavy rainfall.
2. Coal became more valuable after the Industrial Revolution.
3. Wind has greater value in coastal regions for producing electricity than in places with less wind.

2. Why are human beings considered the most important resource for development?

Answer:
Human beings are considered the most important resource because they use their knowledge, skills and technology to develop and utilise other resources wisely. They also create new resources and ensure economic development.

Living resources. Non-living resources.

4. Why is there a need for resource planning? Give any three reasons.

Answer:
1. To ensure proper and balanced use of resources.
2. To prevent over-exploitation and wastage of resources.
3. To conserve resources for future generations and achieve sustainable development.

5. Mention any six factors on which the utilisation of resources depends.

Answer:
1. Availability of resources.
2. Technology.
3. Skilled labour.
4. Capital.
5. Transport and communication.
6. Government policies and planning.

Long Answer 1: Human needs and wants are neither uniform in all parts of the world nor static over the years. Explain.

Answer:
Human needs and wants differ from one place to another because of climate, culture, lifestyle and economic conditions. They also change with time due to technological development and changing living standards. For example, people in cold regions need woollen clothes, while people in hot regions need cotton clothes. Earlier, landline phones were common, but today smartphones and the internet have become essential.

I would prefer renewable resources because they are environment-friendly, reduce pollution and are available for future generations.

Long Answer 3: What is meant by sustainable development? Why is it the need of the hour?

Answer:
Sustainable development means meeting the needs of the present generation without compromising the needs of future generations. It is necessary because natural resources are limited, pollution is increasing, forests are disappearing and environmental balance must be maintained.

Long Answer 4: Describe the various basis of classification of resources with examples.

Answer:
Resources are classified on different bases:

1. On the basis of origin: Biotic and Abiotic resources.
Examples: Plants (biotic), Water (abiotic).

2. On the basis of exhaustibility: Renewable and Non-renewable resources.
Examples: Solar energy, Coal.

3. On the basis of ownership: Individual, Community, National and International resources.

4. On the basis of status of development: Potential, Developed (Actual), Stock and Reserves.
Examples: Wind energy (potential), Coal mines (actual).
